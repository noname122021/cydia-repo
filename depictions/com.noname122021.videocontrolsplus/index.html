VideoControls+
Modern skip & speed controls for iOS 6 media player
Compatibility
iOS 6.0 – 6.1.6
Supported
Features
  • ±10s Quick Skip: Replaces rarely used chapter buttons with instant 10-second rewind and forward controls.
  • Playback Speed Selector: Quick speed HUD (0.5x, 0.75x, 1x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 2x) on the former chapter menu button.
  • Rate Persistence: Preserves chosen speed across pausing, resuming, and scrubbing.
  • Universal Support: Works across Safari, YouTube, Videos app, iFile, and third-party media players.
  • Native Settings Bundle: Configure preferences in Settings > VideoControls+ with master toggle and dynamic third-party app options.
