VideoControls+
Modern skip buttons & speed controls for iOS 6 media player
Features
  • ±10s Quick Skip: Replaces rarely used chapter buttons with quick 10-second rewind and forward buttons.
  • Playback Speed Selector: Instant speed HUD (0.5x, 0.75x, 1x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 2x) on the former chapter menu button.
  • Rate Persistence: Preserves chosen speed across pausing, resuming, and scrubbing.
  • Universal Support: Works across Safari, YouTube, Videos app, and third-party apps using standard MediaPlayer controls.
  • Settings Panel: Master toggle in Settings > VideoControls+ to enable or disable anytime without respring.
