DictaKey v1.0.0
Offline Voice Dictation Keyboard Tweak
▶
Brings completely offline, on-device voice dictation to iOS 6. Injects a skeuomorphic microphone dictation key into the native keyboard, streams low-latency audio into an optimized Vosk Kaldi speech recognition model, and types recognized words into any text input field in real time. Features fast integer VAD, recognizer reuse (~0.5s first-word latency even on iPhone 4), and zero-overhead keyboard typing.
